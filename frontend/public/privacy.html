Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 1, 2026 | Last Updated: September 10, 2026 Active & Compliant

1. Overview & Introduction

Welcome to S4 Automations ("S4 Automations", "we", "us", or "our"), operated by Service4GH. We provide an autonomous financial document orchestration, AI-driven optical character recognition (OCR), and accounting enterprise resource planning (ERP) synchronization suite designed for accounting firms, commercial enterprises, and hospitality organizations.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, process, safeguard, and disclose information when you access or utilize the S4 Automations web application, API endpoints, client portals, and associated communication bots.

Key Guarantee: S4 Automations operates under strict enterprise segregation principles. We do not sell your data, we do not monetize your customer lists, and we do not permit our AI models to train on your proprietary receipts, invoices, or financial records.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information strictly necessary to provide automated bookkeeping, transaction matching, and communication services:

A. Account & Identity Data

  • User Profiles: Name, work email address, telephone/WhatsApp contact number, company or accounting firm name, and designated user roles (e.g. Platform Administrator, Senior Accountant, Client Team Member).
  • Authentication Credentials: Securely hashed passwords, OAuth session tokens, and magic portal access tokens.

B. Financial Documents & Ingestion Files

  • Source Documents: Receipts, vendor invoices, purchase orders, handwritten chits, delivery dockets, and bank / mobile money (MTN MoMo, Telecel Cash) transaction statements uploaded via Google Drive, email, WhatsApp, or direct browser upload.
  • Extracted Accounting Metadata: Line-item descriptions, vendor names, transaction dates, gross amounts, and tax breakdowns (e.g. VAT, NHIL, GETFund, COVID-19 Levy, and Withholding Tax).

C. Connected Third-Party Service Credentials (OAuth)

  • Google Workspace / Google Drive: OAuth tokens enabling read access strictly to user-designated receipt dropboxes and write access to review spreadsheets.
  • Accounting Systems (Zoho Books, QuickBooks Online): Authorized OAuth 2.0 access and refresh tokens utilized strictly to synchronize vendor bills, chart of accounts, expenses, and bank feeds upon user authorization.
  • LinkedIn API (Release Broadcaster): User-authorized OAuth 2.0 tokens (scopes: w_organization_social, w_member_social) used strictly when the user manually initiates a feature release broadcast to their LinkedIn profile or company page. S4 Automations does not read your personal feeds, connections, messages, or private data.
  • Communication Channels (X / Twitter, Mailjet): API keys and Webhook tokens used exclusively for user-directed release publishing and transactional email dispatch.

D. Technical & Diagnostic Telemetry

  • System logs, IP addresses, browser user-agent strings, pipeline execution durations, OCR model confidence metrics, and error stack traces to ensure system stability and audit transparency.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your data is processed exclusively for the following authorized business purposes:

  • Document OCR & Financial Analysis: Converting messy receipt photos and PDF statements into structured double-entry accounting records via Google Gemini Vision models.
  • Staged Review & ERP Posting: Populating private Google Sheets for team review and pushing approved journal entries into your connected accounting software (Zoho Books, QuickBooks Online).
  • Client Queries & Verification: Routing uncategorized bank transactions to designated client contacts via WhatsApp or secure magic links for receipt clarification.
  • Multi-Channel Broadcasts: Publishing public product updates and release notes to authorized channels (LinkedIn, X, Email, In-App Changelog) when instructed by authenticated team members.
  • Security & Fraud Prevention: Detecting duplicate invoice numbers, preventing duplicate payment submissions, and verifying authorization credentials.

4. Third-Party Sub-processors & AI Data Processing

We partner with select, enterprise-grade cloud service providers to power S4 Automations. All sub-processors are bound by strict data protection agreements:

  • Google Cloud & Vertex AI: Cloud hosting, Google Drive API, and Gemini Vision models. Data processed through Google enterprise APIs is encrypted and is never used to train foundation models.
  • Mailjet: Transactional email delivery and automated lead notifications.
  • Twilio: WhatsApp Business messaging infrastructure for inbound receipt submission and query notifications.
  • Vercel & Cloud Infrastructure: High-availability frontend content delivery and encrypted container hosting.

5. Data Security, Retention & Storage

  • Encryption Standards: All data in transit is protected using TLS 1.3 encryption. Sensitive database attributes, API tokens, and client secrets are encrypted at rest using AES-256 standards.
  • Tenant Segregation: Customer organizations, client folders, and financial documents are strictly compartmentalized using row-level security and tenant-scoped database relations.
  • Data Retention: Financial records and audit logs are retained for the duration of your active subscription or as mandated by financial regulatory statutes. Upon client offboarding, account data and cached files can be permanently purged upon written request.

6. User Rights & Data Control

In accordance with the Ghana Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843), the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and applicable international privacy frameworks, you hold the following rights:

  • Access & Portability: The right to view, inspect, and export all records, review sheets, and transactional data stored in S4 Automations.
  • Rectification: The right to correct inaccurate account profiles, vendor records, or tax identification details.
  • Revocation of Third-Party Access: You can disconnect your Google, Zoho Books, QuickBooks, or LinkedIn integrations at any time directly through the platform or via the respective provider's security portal.
  • Erasure: The right to request full deletion of your organization's user accounts and cached files.

7. Communications & Marketing Preferences

Users may opt in to product changelogs, marketing newsletters, and automation best practices. Every marketing email includes a direct, one-click unsubscribe mechanism. System operational alerts (such as pipeline execution failures, urgent clarification requests, and security notifications) cannot be opted out of while maintaining an active account.
